2026 Salon Series · San Francisco · June 8

What can a board do that AI can't?

Thirty nonprofit CEOs, funders, and board chairs spent an afternoon on that question. The first stop of Building the Board Nonprofits Need Now, presented by Board.Dev and Okta.

Watch the highlights

On stage

Three conversations, one question

Buddy Shah, CEO of the Clinton Health Access Initiative and chair of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust, opened the series with Charlotte Wylie of Okta. Hilary Noon of AWS and Michael Lissner of Free Law Project took on what a tech-fluent board looks like in practice. Victor Cordon of Okta closed with how companies can show up for nonprofits.

What we built

Two questions and ten mandates

San Francisco surfaced the two questions the whole series now runs on: what can a board do that AI can't, and how do we finally build the board we've needed for decades? The room drafted ten mandates to answer them. New York pressure-tested those mandates. Chicago extended them.
